About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery dedicated to providing fresh breads, pastries, and cakes to our local community. We believe a neighborhood bakery should be a welcoming place for families, commuters, and friends.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe quality baked goods begin with quality ingredients. We carefully select ingredients for our breads, pastries, and cakes while focusing on freshness and quality.

Meet Our Staff

Our bakery team works together each day to prepare fresh baked goods and provide friendly service to our customers.

Our bakers prepare our breads and pastries, while our customer service team helps customers with orders, questions, and special requests.
